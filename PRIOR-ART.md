

## Search of 15 November 2026 (independent agent, `verification/agent-2026-11-15/REPORT.md`)

Schoenfield's comment in A081978 (25 May 2014) prints f3(p^r) = (p^r - 1)/2. The plus-half form appears for special
cases in Schoenfield's later entries: A340865 (24 Jan 2021, six divisors, with the minus-half case excluded by
divisibility by 4) and A349699 (25 Nov 2021, ten divisors, e.g. 3321 = 3^4 * 41); also A292989 and A398195. Math Stack
Exchange 3103932 (2019) gives the coprime-factor reasoning. The correction is therefore known in special cases; the
general sign error in A081978 was not found corrected anywhere.
